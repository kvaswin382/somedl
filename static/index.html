Transfer limits

KiB/s = kibibytes per second. 1,024 KiB/s is approximately 8.4 Mbps. Limits apply to newly started media jobs.

Appearance & session

This browser stays signed in for 35 days. Signing out revokes its session.

Website cookies

Import your own Netscape-format cookies.txt from your computer. Files stay on this laptop, outside the SSD. Cookie values are never shown in this page.

Website cookies expire independently of your 35-day desk login. Instagram may require fresh cookies even for public posts.

Taking the SSD to Windows?

Hold the queue, then stop the services and safely unmount from the laptop terminal.

sudo systemctl stop zedair zedair-transmission
sudo umount /srv/zedair-ssd

Reconnect: follow the “Using the SSD in Windows” steps in README.md. No formatting is needed.
